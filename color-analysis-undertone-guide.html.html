Skin Undertone & Color Guide

Your skin tone changes with the sun, but your undertone remains exactly the same forever. Aligning your wardrobe to your undertone guarantees you always look clean, radiant, and intentional.

How to Discover Your Skin Profile

Check 01: Wrist Vein Evaluation

Examine the color of your primary inner veins under raw daylight:

Green / Olive Shades = Warm Base
Blue / Purple Shades = Cool Base
Blended / Teal Shades = Neutral Base

Check 02: Metallic Contrast Response

Hold standard polished jewelry elements next to your jawline in a mirror:

Gold Illuminates Best = Warm Profile
Silver Casts Cleaner Glow = Cool Profile
Balanced Affinity to Both = Neutral Profile
